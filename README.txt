ERNDUB AUDIO – funky + animated skins integrated
================================================

Files:
  ernamp.html   – your player with animated skin CSS inserted in <style>
                  and changeSkin() fixed to clear anim-* classes on switch
  skins.js      – original skins + 17 new funky skins (stripey/gradient/dotty/animated)
  animated-skins.css – same keyframes standalone (already inlined in ernamp.html)

Usage:
  Put ernamp.html + skins.js (and your other scripts: stations.js, visualizers.js,
  games.js, utilities.js) in the same folder and open ernamp.html.

New skins appear in the skin selector dropdown automatically.
Animated ones: Pulse Neon, Rainbow Flow, Wave Drift, Glitter Bomb, Matrix Rain
